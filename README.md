# 🏮 Aburaya Grand Bathhouse & Spirit Spa (油屋)

> *"Where Eight Million Gods and wandering spirits wash away the sludge of the mortal realm."*

An expansive, atmospheric, story-driven single-page business platform for **Aburaya (油屋)**, Madam Yubaba's grand imperial spirit bathhouse inspired by Studio Ghibli's *Spirited Away*.

---

## ✨ Features

- **🏯 Ambient Twilight Realm Atmosphere:** Floating spirit glow orbs, gold accents, and Japanese aesthetic typography (*Noto Serif JP*, *Cinzel Decorative*).
- **♨️ Sacred Thermal Springs (2x2 Grid):** Showcase of imperial chambers including the Sovereign River God Chamber, Kamaji's Volcanic Sulfur Vat, and Oshira-sama’s White Lotus Steam Hall.
- **🏷️ Kamaji's Subterranean Bath Tokens:** Interactive token selection routing herbal boiler lines (Wild Wormwood, Lotus & Pearl, Bamboo & Pine, Dragon Gold Leaf).
- **🍡 Post-Purification Banquet Hall:** Spirit menu featuring honeyed red bean buns, Haku’s herbal onigiri, steaming dumplings, and camellia dewdrop tea.
- **📜 Divine Patron Scrolls:** Customer testimonials from iconic deities (*Lord Okami*, *No-Face (Kaonashi)*, and *The Radish Spirit*).
- **🪶 Interactive Spirit Ledger Form:** Booking reservation system stamped with Madam Yubaba's wax seal.
- **📱 Fully Responsive:** Optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.

---

## 🛠️ Built With

- **HTML5** – Semantic, accessible structure
- **CSS3** – Modern Flexbox & CSS Grid, custom properties, animations & gradients
- **Font Awesome 6** – Thematic icons
- **Google Fonts** – *Cinzel Decorative*, *Noto Serif JP*, *Plus Jakarta Sans*, *JetBrains Mono*
